By Journal

I have organized by journal all the articles on disability and accessibility that I could find in the journals listed in the methods. The articles are organized chronologically within each journal category.

Observing the number of articles appearing in the four journals with special issues, we notice that, although BPCQ and IEEE have numerous articles on disability, those large numbers are mostly the result of having published two special issues on the topic. Outside of those issues, these journals have not published regular articles on this topic. BPCQ only has two articles on disability before and four articles after its 2018 special issues. In IEEE, of the 21 articles dedicated to the topic, there are only six articles that appear outside of the two special issues. TC has the smallest number of articles on this topic, and it waited 14 years to republish on the topic of disability after its 2006 special issue. Unfortunately, due to the journal ending its run, we will not find any more articles on this topic from TC. Contrastingly, after a late start (also due to a later publication launch than the other journals—starting in 2001), CDQ has fewer articles in its special issue but a larger number of articles that appear consistently throughout its publication history.
