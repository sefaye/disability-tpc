List of all the articles focusing on older adults that I could find in the journals listed in the methods.

  • Lippincott, Gail (2004). Gray matters: Where are the technical communicators in research and design for aging audiences? IEEE Transactions on Professional Communication, 47(3), 157–170.

  • Van Der Meij, Hans, & Gellevij, Mark. (2002). Effects of Pictures, Age, and Experience on Learning to Use a Computer Program. Technical Communication, 49(3), 330–339.
